## What NOT to Buy (Yet)

- **Shopify:** $39/mo minimum + transaction fees. Good platform, but not necessary when you're starting. Migrate later when revenue justifies it.
- **Premium themes:** Most are slower than free alternatives. Buy after you have traffic data.
- **Managed WordPress hosting:** $25+/mo. Hostinger's shared plan handles 10K monthly visitors fine.
- **Email marketing tools:** Mailchimp free tier (500 contacts) is enough for your first 6 months.

## The Real Secret

Your first 12 months are about selling, not optimizing infrastructure.

When a customer asks "where's my order," they don't care about your tech stack. They care about a fast, working website and a quick reply. This $2.99/month setup delivers both.

As you grow, upgrade one layer at a time: CDN first, then managed hosting, then custom automation. But don't pre-buy capacity you don't need yet.
