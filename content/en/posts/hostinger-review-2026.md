## Who This Is For

If you're running a cross-border store with a team of 1 to 5 people, you don't need a $50/month cloud server. You need something that loads fast, doesn't crash during promotions, and won't eat into your ad budget.

I tested Hostinger's entry plans on a real WooCommerce store. Here's what I found.

## Plans That Actually Matter

Hostinger has four tiers. For small cross-border teams, only two are relevant:

| Plan | Price (48-month) | Worth It? |
|---|---|---|
| Premium | $2.99/mo | ✅ Sweet spot. 100 websites, free SSL, weekly backups |
| Business | $3.99/mo | ✅ If you need daily backups + CDN |
| Cloud | $8.99/mo | ⚠️ Overkill for most small stores |

Skip "Single Shared" — no free domain and only one website. The savings aren't worth it.

## Real Performance Test

I deployed an identical WooCommerce store with 20 products, Astra theme, and standard plugins (Stripe, PayPal, Yoast SEO). No CDN. Tested from 5 locations.

**Load Time:**
- US East: 1.2s
- London: 1.5s
- Singapore: 2.1s
- São Paulo: 2.8s

For stores targeting US/European customers, performance is excellent. Southeast Asia and South America see noticeable slowdown — add Cloudflare CDN if those are your markets.

**Uptime:** 99.95% over 30 days. One brief outage (4 minutes) during off-peak hours.

**Support Response:** Live chat answered in under 2 minutes across 5 test contacts. Agents are competent for hosting issues, but don't expect WooCommerce-specific debugging.

## What Hostinger Gets Right

- **hPanel** is genuinely cleaner than cPanel. If you've never managed hosting before, you'll find your way around in 10 minutes.
- **LiteSpeed Cache** is pre-installed. For WooCommerce, this alone can cut load times by 40-60% versus Apache-based hosts.
- **Free SSL + domain** on Premium and above. One less thing to worry about.
- **WordPress one-click install** works. Not groundbreaking, but it doesn't break.

## Where Hostinger Falls Short

- **No phone support.** Live chat is fast, but when your site goes down during a flash sale, typing feels slow.
- **Renewal pricing jumps.** Premium renews at $7.99/mo. Either commit to a long term or set a calendar reminder to reassess before renewal.
- **Resource limits are real.** The "unlimited" bandwidth has I/O caps. If you're running heavy plugins or get a traffic spike, you'll hit them. Plan accordingly or upgrade to Cloud.

## The Honest Verdict

**Use Hostinger if:**
- Your team is 1-5 people
- You're targeting US/European customers
- You want "set it and forget it" hosting
- You don't have a dedicated developer

**Skip Hostinger if:**
- You need to run Docker containers or custom backend services
- Your primary market is Asia or South America (without CDN planning)
- You're doing $500K+/year and can justify managed cloud hosting

At $2.99/month, it's the best value in shared hosting for small cross-border teams. Is it the fastest? No. Is it the most cost-effective thing that works reliably? Yes.
